## Public wording guardrail

Safe before validation:

> Responsible downstream routing is available when required.

Safe after partner evidence is on file:

> For material that requires certified recycling or destruction, Sentinel can route assets through validated downstream partners and provide available partner documentation.

Avoid until contract/evidence exists:

- “Sentinel is R2v3 certified.”
- “NAID AAA certified destruction.”
- “Zero landfill.”
- Naming a partner as if they endorse Sentinel.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Screenshot/PDF of current certification from SERI/R2 directory or cert body.
- [ ] Accepted material list.
- [ ] Business account / third-party harvester acceptance.
- [ ] Certificate of recycling/destruction sample.
- [ ] Pricing or revenue-share terms.

- [ ] Public wording permission if naming partner.
