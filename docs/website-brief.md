# Sentinel ITAD Website Brief

**Project:** sentinelitad.com public lead-generation site  
**Business:** Growth Web Development LLC DBA Sentinel IT Asset Disposal  
**Base:** Meridian, Idaho / Treasure Valley  
**Primary contact for now:** Michael@growthwebdev.com · 808-498-1125

## Website job

The site needs to do three things:

1. Make Sentinel look safer and more professional than a junk hauler.
2. Capture pickup/bin-placement/decommissioning leads from local MSPs and businesses.
3. Avoid compliance overclaims until the tooling, insurance, certificates, and downstream evidence are real.

## Best current positioning

> Secure local IT asset recovery for Treasure Valley businesses: retired servers, workstations, networking gear, storage media, and office technology handled with documented intake, data-media awareness, value recovery, and responsible downstream routing.

Tone: small, exclusive, security-first, sober, direct. Not cute. Not “we love junk.”

## Target audiences

| Audience | Pain | Site promise |
|---|---|---|
| MSPs | Storage rooms filling with retired client hardware | Free/recurring pickup or feeder bin, easy handoff, no awkward disposal pile |
| SMB offices | Old computers and servers sitting around | Local pickup, accountable operator, responsible handling |
| Law/medical/finance firms | Liability from data-bearing devices | Chain-of-custody intake and clear media-handling/disposition path |
| Engineering/CAD/VFX/AI shops | Workstations/GPUs/servers retire but still have value | Value recovery and secure disposition instead of blind scrap |
