Service notes and limitations

Sentinel IT Asset Disposal is operated by Growth Web Development LLC in Meridian, Idaho. This site describes intended services and intake workflows for local IT asset recovery. Exact scope, pricing, documentation, and handling requirements are confirmed per job.

Data-bearing media

Storage media may require special handling, return, sanitization, physical destruction, or downstream partner processing. Sentinel will not claim a sanitization or destruction event occurred unless it is documented for that job.

Certification claims

Unless explicitly documented in a job-specific agreement, Sentinel does not claim to be R2v3, NAID, or otherwise certified as a final electronics recycler or destruction facility. End-of-life recycling and destruction may be routed through qualified downstream partners.

Restricted materials

CRT displays, leaking/swollen batteries, chemicals, medical devices with patient data, classified/government media, and hazardous materials require prior written approval or may be refused.
