Simple operating flow

From retired hardware to clean disposition.

  1. Scope

    You tell us what you have: counts, asset classes, media sensitivity, stairs/loading constraints, and timing.

  2. Pickup

    We schedule local pickup or bin placement and record the custody handoff for sensitive jobs. See the process.

  3. Intake

    Assets are sorted into reuse, resale, part-out, internal-use, data-media, and downstream recycling paths.

  4. Disposition

    You receive the appropriate record: pickup receipt, asset/media log, sanitization/destruction evidence, or downstream partner documentation.

Accepted material

Good fits

  • Switches, firewalls, routers, access points

Not a fit without prior discussion

Restricted

  • CRT monitors or televisions
  • Swollen/leaking batteries or hazardous chemicals
  • Medical devices with patient data still present
  • Classified/government media requiring special handling
