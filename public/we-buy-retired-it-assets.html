How it works

Buyout and cleanout, handled together.

  1. Scope

    You tell us what's retiring: rough counts, asset classes, media sensitivity, stairs and loading constraints, timing.

  2. Offer

    We price what we're buying and quote the pickup for the rest — one number for the buyout, one clear plan for the cleanout.

  3. Pickup

    We schedule local pickup, record the custody handoff, and separate data-bearing media on site where the job requires it.

  4. Records

    You get pickup receipts and the appropriate disposition records: asset/media logs, sanitization or destruction evidence, downstream documentation.
