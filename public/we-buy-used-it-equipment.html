What we pay for

Good fits

  • Rack and tower servers (Dell, HPE, Supermicro, Lenovo)
  • Workstations, desktops, laptops, thin clients
  • Switches, firewalls, routers, access points
  • RAM, CPUs, SSDs, NVMe drives, NICs, HBAs, GPUs
  • Enterprise storage arrays and disk shelves
  • Flat-panel monitors in working condition

Not a fit

We pass on

  • CRT monitors or televisions
  • Consumer printers and ink/toner
  • Broken beyond repair or missing major parts
  • Swollen/leaking batteries or hazardous material

How selling to us works

From dusty closet to cash, in four steps.

  1. Tell us what you have

    Counts, models, condition, and your location. Photos help but aren't required.

  2. Get an offer

    A straight number, fast. No lowball games, no "we'll see when we get there."

  3. We pick up

    Local pickup across the Treasure Valley. Drives with your data are separated and handled on your terms — wiped, destroyed, or returned to you.

  4. You get paid

    Payment on pickup. The gear gets tested and put back to work instead of gathering dust.
