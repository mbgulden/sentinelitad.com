What we pay for

Good fits

  • DDR5 ECC RDIMM (4800+)
  • DDR4 ECC RDIMM and LRDIMM (2133–3200)
  • DDR3 ECC RDIMM (1066–1866)
  • Desktop DDR4/DDR5 and laptop SODIMM in working pulls
  • Bulk lots from decommissions — matched sets a plus
  • New/sealed sticks at a discount to retail

Not a fit

We pass on

  • Non-ECC desktop memory older than DDR3
  • Known-bad or untested-for-parts sticks (we test, but price accordingly)
  • Counterfeit or remarked modules

How it works

Sticks to cash.

  1. Tell us the specs

    Capacity, speed, ECC type, and quantity. A photo of the labels is ideal — part numbers tell us everything.

    Every stick is tested in-house before final pricing. What passes, we buy.

  4. You get paid

    Local pickup or drop-off in Meridian. Payment on the spot.
